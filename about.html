We are Dream Team.

Our team consists of 6 members, each one of us is unique and talented in their own way. Let's get to know each other better! Our names are Iliana Androutsopoulou, Konstantina Kontoleon, Dimitra Androutsopoulou, Valentini Mathioudaki, Katerina and Ioanna Kontaki. We signed up for the 'Code Like a Girl' program because we wanted to learn more about coding and how to create a project that can help people improve their digital health. We believe that taking care of our digital health is just as important as taking care of our physical and mental health, and we wanted to create a project that would raise awareness about this issue. We hope that our project will inspire others to take care of their digital health and make positive changes in people's lives.

We are excited to share our project with you and hope you find it helpful!
