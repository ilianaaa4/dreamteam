Welcome

Welcome to Sweet Dreams, a website dedicated to helping you improve your sleep quality and well-being. We understand how important it is to get a good night's sleep, especially in today's hectic world. Our goal is to provide you with the resources, tips and information you need to improve your sleep habits to the best of your ability under the act of Digital Wellbeing.

What is Digital Wellbeing?

Cited by many experts and organizations, digital wellbeing is defined as the act of utilizing technology to a safe extent, with the goal of improving the overall quality of the user’s life. Common examples include built in tools on devices such as app timers, various modes that silence notifications and pause distracting apps (eg. Social media), as well as actions revolving around digital detoxification.

Are sleep quality and digital wellbeing correlated?

While overall causes may vary between each user, strong correlation between high screen times and poor sleep quality, oftentimes chronic, has been indicated by various studies. When something as crucial as sleep quality is impacted by a person's use of technology, it is important for digital boundaries to be set in place.

What are some common signs of screen addiction?

  • Visible irritation and changes in mood when forced to stop using a device
  • Eye strain, blurred vision, dry eyes, etc.
  • Chronic neck or back pain caused by poor posture
  • Sleep disturbances due to late night device use
  • Isolation from friends and family in favor of virtual connections

DISCLAIMER: While those symptoms are linked with screen addiction, it is important to verify the source of the problem by talking to medical and psychological experts.
