Tips and Tricks

Improving Your Sleep Quality...

  1. Keep a consistent sleep schedule

    2. Going to bed and waking up at the same time every day can help regulate your body's internal clock, so that you avoid headaches or other body issues when online! You can organise the hours you sleep depending on your daily activities. For example, an adult needs 7-9 hours of sleep per night, teens need 8-10 hours, while children need 9-11 hours to feel well-rested.

  2. Create a relaxing bedtime routine
  3. Make your bedroom comfortable and dark
  4. Avoid screens before bedtime
  5. Limit caffeine and alcohol intake
