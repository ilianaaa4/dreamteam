Tips and Tricks

Improving Your Sleep Quality...

  1. Keep a consistent sleep schedule

    2. Going to bed and waking up at the same time every day can help regulate your body's internal clock, so that you avoid headaches or other body issues when online! You can organise the hours you sleep depending on your daily activities. For example, an adult needs 7-9 hours of sleep per night, teens need 8-10 hours, while children need 9-11 hours to feel well-rested.

  2. Avoid screens before bedtime and after waking up

    3. It is advisabble to avoid the usage of electronic devices 30 minutes before you go to bed and after waking up. There is no need to trigure your brain in the time of the day you have to relax. Scrolling in social media, playing games, or continuing to work keeps your mind alert while it is supposed to wind down.

  3. Keep devices out of the bedroom

    4. Electronic devices emit blue light that interfere with your body's production of melatonin, a hormone that regulates sleep. Keeping them out of the bedroom can help create a more conducive environment for sleep.

In your Daily Life...

  1. Manage notifications

    2. During the day a large number of notifications distracts your brain, resulting in poor performaces at work or school. By deactivating unnecessary notifications, such as advertisements, you manage to concentrate better when needed.

  2. Keep it simple!

    3. A simple layout in your electronic devices helps you concentrate more on finding directly what you need rather than unlocking the device and immediately being "caught" by xthe layout. It is an unessesary distraction to you. So you'd rather keep it simple to improve your experience with your digital devices!

  3. Adopt the 20-20-20 rule

    4. Every 20 minutes, take a 20-second break and look at something 20 feet away. This can help reduce eye strain and improve focus.

With family...

  1. Interact via collaborative platforms with family and friends

    2. Using collaborative platforms can help you stay connected with family and friends, fostering stronger relationships and providing support when needed.

  2. The use of "device parking"

    3. An idea to stay offfline while with family is to set a basket next to the front door, so that everytime a family member returns to home, he will have to put all electronic devces to the basket. Using that way you will be able to spend time with your familly without any distractions.
